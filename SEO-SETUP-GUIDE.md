1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)

3. Verify ownership (use HTML tag method or DNS)
4. Submit sitemap: `https://dentistrygrowthpartners.com/sitemap-index.xml`
5. Request indexing for homepage and key pages

#### 2. **Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools**
1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)
2. Add site: `https://dentistrygrowthpartners.com`
3. Verify ownership
4. Submit sitemap: `https://dentistrygrowthpartners.com/sitemap-index.xml`

#### 3. **Create Google Business Profile**
1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)
2. Create/claim your business listing
3. Add complete information (address, phone, hours, services)
4. Add photos and videos
5. Link to your website
6. Get reviews from satisfied clients

#### 4. **Submit to IndexNow**
IndexNow allows instant notification to search engines when content changes.
```bash
# Submit your sitemap to IndexNow (Bing, Yandex, etc.)
curl -X POST "https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow?url=https://dentistrygrowthpartners.com/sitemap-index.xml&key=YOUR_API_KEY"
```
